## 有什么好习惯对你的学习影响大的
Three daily habits have helped me study the most.

First, I make a daily study plan every night before bed to map out what I’ll learn the next day.

Second, I review what I’ve learned each day right before sleeping.

Finally, I keep building up my knowledge by jotting down any new information I come across.

> 第一是坚持制定每日学习计划。每天睡前简单规划第二天的学习任务。
第二是坚持总结。每天睡前总结今天学习的知识点。
最后是坚持积累。遇到新知识把它积累下来。

## 你本科感觉压力最大的是什么时候？
> When did you feel the most stressed during your undergraduate studies?



我感觉压力最大的时候是准备考试的时候。因为考试周通常有复习内容比较多且时间短。除此之外我非常担心不能通过考试。每当这个时候我都尽我最大的努力去准备考试。

>  The time I felt the most stressed was exam preparation. There was always a huge amount of material to review in a short time during exam week. Besides, I was constantly worried about failing the exams. Whenever this happened, I tried my best to prepare thoroughly.
