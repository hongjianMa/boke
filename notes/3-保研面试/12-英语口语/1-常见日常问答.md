## 有什么好习惯对你的学习影响大的
Three daily habits have helped me study the most.

First, I make a daily study plan every night before bed to map out what I’ll learn the next day.

Second, I review what I’ve learned each day right before sleeping.

Finally, I keep building up my knowledge by jotting down any new information I come across.

> 第一是坚持制定每日学习计划。每天睡前简单规划第二天的学习任务。
第二是坚持总结。每天睡前总结今天学习的知识点。
最后是坚持积累。遇到新知识把它积累下来。
